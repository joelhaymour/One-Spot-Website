# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses (trades and field service, clinics, dealerships, financing, distribution, local services) who are not technical. They run on a handful of tools that don't talk to each other, so their team retypes the same details, chases things by email and finds problems late. They arrive at the site wondering whether "modern tools" could help and where they would even start, and they decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

One Spot is a consulting service, not a software product. A small team learns how a particular business really works, finds where time leaks between people and tools, and builds the fix for that business: connecting the tools it already uses, taking repetitive work off the team, and giving the owner a clear view. Website redesigns are a second, smaller offer. Success is an owner who gets in touch through the contact form.

## Positioning

Every build is custom and tailored to one business and its workflows. There are no pre-made demos, templates or "default models": a demo is built for a prospect from their own answers, usually by the next day. The work starts by understanding the business first, then technology. What the visitor should leave wanting is to fix how work flows in their business; websites come second.

## Operating Context

- The website (this project, Next.js, live at www.onespotgroup.com) feeds the One Spot HUD, the team's internal tool: an enquiry creates an account, the HUD researches the business and drafts a reply for a person to send.
- General enquiries are offered "a demo by tomorrow" (a few quick questions, then a custom demo built from the answers). Website redesign enquiries get two style concepts of their own site as the first reply.
- Nothing is sent to a prospect automatically; a person reviews every email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Copy is US English, plain words a busy owner would use. No jargon, no "AI-powered", no futurism, no AI or robot imagery.
- Examples must be operational (parts, claims, invoices, warranty credits, scheduling), never marketing.
- Dollar figures for recovered time are "potential value", never promised savings.
- Pricing is not published.
- Motion is progressive: content stays visible without JavaScript and with reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: One Spot. Mark: a solid spot inside a ring.
- Colour and type identity carried over from the HUD (warm paper, ink, one blue spot; Inter) unless the owner replaces it.
- The company speaks as "we".

## Evidence on Hand

- Real website redesigns for two small online stores: Rouqe Golf and Strawberry Milk Mob. The owner allows naming them, but they are small and website-only, so they should not be presented as workflow or automation proof.
- No testimonials, client logos, case-study numbers or published demos yet. Every business example on the site is illustrative and must be labelled as such. Do not invent quotes, clients, results or metrics.
- A small team is behind One Spot; names, photos and a location are not provided yet.

## Product Principles

1. Business first, technology second: show understanding of the owner's work before any tool.
2. Custom over catalogue: never present a build as a product someone could pick off a shelf.
3. People stay in charge: the owner decides, and a person reviews anything that goes out.
4. Plain and honest: illustrative stays labelled, potential value stays "potential".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast and controls; autoplaying content must be pausable; reduced-motion visitors get the finished states.
