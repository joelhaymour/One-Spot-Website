## 4. HOW I WORK

1. **Learn the business.** I sit with you and your team. How the money comes in, where the time goes.
2. **Find the friction.** Where people wait, retype, chase and forget. Ranked by what it costs you.
3. **Draw the org chart.** Which agents, which jobs, what needs your sign-off. On one page, before anything is built.
4. **Build one, prove it.** One department first. It proves itself on your real work before the next begins.
5. **Connect everything.** Agents plug into the software you already pay for, and report to one CEO Agent.
6. **Keep it sharp.** Every month: what the agents did, what they missed, what to build next.

## 5. DEPARTMENT ONE-LINERS

- **CEO Agent.** Sees every department. Tells you what matters today, and what to do about it.
- **Marketing Agent.** Tracks what brings in customers and moves budget toward it.
- **Finance Agent.** Chases invoices, matches payments, and warns you before cash gets tight.
- **Sales Agent.** Follows up every lead within minutes. Nobody goes cold by accident.
- **Customer Service Agent.** Answers the routine. Hands your people the rest. Nothing sits unread.
- **Operations Agent.** Schedules the work, checks capacity, spots the delay before your customer does.
- **Internal Knowledge Agent.** Knows every policy, price and past job. Answers your team in seconds.
- **Administration Agent.** Handles the calendar, the paperwork and the renewals nobody remembers.

## 6. CEO AGENT RECOMMENDATIONS

1. `CUSTOMER SERVICE` First-reply time is up 31% this month, from 3h 12m to 4h 12m. 64% of messages are repeat questions. **I recommend deploying a Customer Support Agent for first replies.**
2. `CASH FLOW` At the current pace, cash falls below your $40,000 floor on the 19th. Six customers owe $31,500 that is already due. **I recommend the Finance Agent chase those six today.** Reminders are drafted for your approval.
3. `SALES` 14 quotes worth $86,400 have had no follow-up in 7 days. Your win rate halves after day five. **I recommend the Sales Agent follow up all 14 today.** Drafts are ready.
4. `REVENUE` Revenue is at 58% of monthly target with 8 working days left. 3 deals worth $41,000 are each waiting on one signature. Closing them brings the month to 92%. **I recommend you call these three today.**
5. `MARKETING / OPERATIONS` Enquiries are up 46% in six days, from the new campaign. The schedule is 88% booked for three weeks. Operations can open 12 more slots. **I recommend opening them before raising ad spend.**
6. `CAPACITY` The Marketing Agent's workload has tripled in 14 days. It reports it is becoming the bottleneck. **I recommend splitting it into three specialists: research, execution, reporting.**
7. `INTERNAL KNOWLEDGE` Your team asked the same 23 questions 212 times this month. 9 have no written answer. **I recommend deploying an Internal Knowledge Agent, and 40 minutes of your time to answer the nine.**
8. `ADMINISTRATION` 38 purchases are waiting on your approval. Typical wait is 2.3 days, and 31 of them are under $2,000. **I recommend raising the auto-approve limit to $2,000, with a daily summary from the Administration Agent.**

Action labels: `Approve` · `Review` · `Not now`

The figures form one consistent company:
- The monthly target is $120,000 and revenue booked is $69,600 (58%). Adding $41,000 brings it to 92%.
- The revenue tile should read $69,600 / $120,000.
- Item 5 matches the network relay strings.
- Item 6 matches the scaling scene.
- Item 1 is the default recommendation on load.

## WHY THE HERO HEADLINE WINS

- **Only this company can run it.** The brand name is the promise, so the line can't appear on any other AI agency's site.
- **It captions what is on screen.** The visitor reads "Your whole business. One spot." while looking at a whole business on one display. No other candidate matched the picture, and it contains no AI word for the visitor to distrust.
- **It passes the dentist test in five words and leaves the explaining to the subhead.**
  - "Business" covers a practice, a contractor and a corporation alike.
  - "The operating layer for your company" is the positioning, but it needs teaching first. That job goes to the eyebrow and quiet moment 1, where the term is defined by the owner's own condition.
