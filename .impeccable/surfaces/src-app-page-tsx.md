# Homepage (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the one-page site at www.onespotgroup.com. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: non-technical owners of small and mid-sized businesses (trades, clinics, dealerships, financing, local services). Job: decide whether One Spot understands their business well enough to get in touch. Action: the contact form (general help, or website redesign), reached from the hero calculator's stub and the nav.
Proof on hand: illustrative operational examples only (labelled); no testimonials, logos or real metrics.
Constraints: keep all current content and functions (worth calculator, Business first process, Your tools scroll scene, four business examples, FAQ, contact form with website mode and demo offer). Reduced motion and no-JS see finished states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the job ticket that follows work through a shop: filled in once, stamped as it moves. It refuses the category default of a cream consultancy page with a product hub diagram, blue accent headlines and eyebrow labels over identical text-left/card-right sections. Where the owner asked for the tools to be shown connected (their words for Your tools, 2026-10-03: "Throughout the animation, it shows One Spot connecting them or allowing them to communicate, so fewer clicks happen."), the centre is the job's own ticket, filled in once, never a product node.

OWN-WORLD: Manila tag stock owns the ground; paper-white tickets sit on it with a reinforced eyelet; ink rules, ruled fields with units, red stamp ink for problems, One Spot blue rationed to its own stamp, the action and after states; carbon-copy tints (canary, pink, green, blue-white) mark each kind of business. Industrial condensed display caps (Big Shoulders Display) over Inter body. Square-ish 4px corners, stamped rectangles, perforated tear lines. The densest passage (the examples) sits on the shop counter: ink ground, tickets laid on it. Apps are drawn in ink on paper, never in their own brand colours; in Your tools the connected state centres on the job's own ticket ("filled in once, set up by One Spot"), not a product node.

STORY: The visitor sees their own week as a ticket, puts in their team and hours, and the yearly hours land as a stamp. They read how we work as a routing slip, watch one job stop bouncing between apps, see a job ticket from a business like theirs today and with One Spot, then fill in a ticket of their own.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: headline in huge condensed caps, lead, primary blue action "Start with a conversation" and a secondary. Right: a hanging job ticket (string, eyelet, 2 degree tilt) that is the calculator: title, Team / Just me tabs, three ruled fields with sliders, the result as a red stamp, ruled lines for value and workweeks, the owner-specified statement that switches with the tabs ("Imagine what your team could accomplish with that time back." / "Imagine what you could do if you had that time back."), the potential-value note line, and a perforated tear-off stub carrying "You don't always need more people" and the "Talk these numbers through" action.

FORM: The Job Ticket, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list (seed:5bf83c7ab0f036f8). Signature interaction: the stamp, landing with one press when a result settles and then still. Raises: stamps land once and stay still (passport); blue only on what One Spot changes (Rietveld); carbon tint per business (city fold); every number in a ruled field with its unit (oscilloscope); one motion clock (cracktro); content on the top sheet, copies beneath as depth (multiplane).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
