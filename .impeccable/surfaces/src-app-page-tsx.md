# Homepage (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the one-page site at www.onespotgroup.com. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: non-technical owners of small and mid-sized businesses. Job: decide whether One Spot understands their business well enough to get in touch. Action: the contact form (general help, or website redesign), reached from the hero calculator's action and the nav.
Proof on hand: illustrative operational examples only (labelled); no testimonials, logos or real metrics.
Constraints: keep all current content and functions; the headline "Modern workflows. Made simple." and the calculator copy are the owner's. Reduced motion and no-JS see finished states.
History: a bolder "job ticket" world (manila, stamps, condensed caps) was built and rejected by the owner on 2026-10-18 as hard to read and unprofessional; a safer re-roll offered the current look sharpened, a drafting-sheet look and a friendly white look, and the owner chose the current look sharpened.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The incumbent One Spot page, sharpened: calm, readable and professional, one blue spot that marks only what matters. It refuses blue-accented headlines, eyebrow labels, decorative glows and grey text below AA.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper #f5f2ec ground, white cards with soft shadows and 22-24px corners, ink #15171b type with a darker secondary grey (#5d636c), Inter set heavy and tight for headings. Blue #2d4ae0 is reserved for the hero's result number, the One Spot side of every comparison, the mark and focus. Pills and rounded toggles with fast (about 200ms) movement.

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, works out what better working could be worth for their own team, and can carry those numbers straight into the contact form. They see how we work, watch one job stop bouncing between apps, compare an ordinary job in a business like theirs today and with One Spot side by side, and get in touch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: "Modern workflows. Made simple." in ink, the owner's lead, the primary "Start with a conversation" and "See how we work", and one reassurance line naming who it's for. Right: the calculator card: question, My Team's Time / My Time toggle, three sliders, the yearly hours as the one blue number, value and workweeks beneath, the owner's statement line, the potential-value note, and a footer band with the closing couplet and "Talk these numbers through".

FORM: Current look, sharpened (grounded, safer re-roll round 1; seed:5bf83c7ab0f036f8). Signature interaction: the calculator's numbers glide to their new values and carry into the contact form. Examples: both sides at once on wide screens, one at a time on phones, slower, pausable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
